Vue adaptation

Import TabsRoot, TabsList, TabsTab, and TabsPanel from @vyrnforge/vue/tabs.

Selection and composition

Give each tab/panel pair the same value. Bind controlled selection with modelValue/update:modelValue or use the documented default-value prop for uncontrolled initialization. Configure orientation and activation on the root.

Accessibility and hydration

Give TabsList an accessible name. Vue renders the tab relationships while the VyrnForge behavior contract owns roving focus, disabled skipping, direction-aware keyboard movement, and activation mode.
