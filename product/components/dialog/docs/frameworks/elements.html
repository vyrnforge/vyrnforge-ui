Custom Elements adaptation

Import defineDialogElement from @vyrnforge/elements/dialog, load the Dialog stylesheet, and register <vf-dialog> explicitly.

Composition and state

Place a native <dialog> inside <vf-dialog> and mark title, description, body, and close content with the documented data-vf-dialog-* hooks. The open property/attribute controls visibility and backdrop-dismiss opts into backdrop dismissal. Listen for cancelable vf-dismiss-request and committed vf-open-change.

Accessibility and upgrade

Registration upgrades authored markup, connects title/description relationships, activates modal behavior, and restores focus after close. Do not rely on cross-framework hydration semantics for the custom-element surface.
