Expose an opt-in, framework-neutral CSS Motion utility with fade, grow, slide, zoom, fade-slide, and pop entrance recipes through `@vyrnforge/web/styles/motion.css` and the existing foundation. Motion uses public timing/easing tokens, logical-direction-aware slide positioning and automatic reduced-motion suppression; it does not manage component presence, exit lifecycles, or focus.
